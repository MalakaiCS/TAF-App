SumatraPDF
----------
This application bundles SumatraPDF.exe (SumatraPDF 3.4.6, 32-bit) and uses it
to print order PDFs directly to a printer without requiring a separate PDF
viewer.

ZXing ("Zebra Crossing") for JavaScript
----------------------------------------
The web/phone app (docs/app/vendor/zxing.min.js) includes @zxing/library
0.21.3, unmodified, to read QR codes and barcodes through the phone's camera
on browsers that cannot read them themselves (every iPhone).
